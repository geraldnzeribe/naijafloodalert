      How it works

      1. Forecast: daily river discharge for the next 30 days from the Copernicus GloFAS hydrological model (via the open Open-Meteo Flood API).
      2. Local baseline: each point's forecast is compared with that river's own daily record since 2010. Days above the 90th percentile trigger Watch, above the 98th Warning, above the 99.7th Severe.
      3. Rainfall boost: heavy 7-day rainfall (≥ 100 mm) raises the level by one step for urban flash-flood sites, where small drainage channels respond faster than big rivers.
      4. Plain-language alert: the result is turned into a short message in English or Pidgin with the expected peak date and safety actions, ready to send by WhatsApp or SMS.

      Prototype for research and demonstration. It does not replace official warnings from NIHSA, NEMA or State Emergency Management Agencies. Always follow their instructions.
